About Elite Camp

Elite Camp is a competitive soccer camp for boys and girls ages 8–17, run as either a day camp or an overnight stay on campus.

Days are built around two long field sessions, with position-specific instruction and games that count. Awards are given at the end of the week to top campers in several categories, and college coaches speak to the group on special topics each afternoon.

Campers are supervised throughout by the coaching staff.

The week includes

  • Two field sessions a day, Monday to Wednesday
  • Position-specific instruction
  • Goalkeeper School alongside the field sessions
  • College coaches speaking on special topics
  • Awards for top campers in several categories
  • Overnight option in air-conditioned dorms

Goalkeeper School

Goalkeeping is coached properly here, not squeezed into the last ten minutes of a field session.

The Goalkeeper School runs during Elite Camp, with dedicated instruction for keepers on technique and the demands of the position. Keepers choose how much of the week they spend in goal.

  • Full-time goalkeeper training for keepers who want it
  • Part-time keepers choose daily between the field session and the keeper session
  • Position-specific technical instruction
