About team camp

This camp is built for high school girls soccer teams who want real preseason preparation: competitive games, proper training volume and time together away from the usual routine.

Teams register as a unit rather than as individual players, and train under the Maryville College coaching staff alongside their own coaches.

Each team receives

  • Two training sessions per day
  • Games every evening
  • A college-level coaching environment
  • Optional team-building experiences on campus

Optional add-on

Maryville College Mountain Challenge

Team-building activities led by Maryville College Mountain Challenge in the college’s award-winning campus woods.

High ropes

Elements up in the trees, with the whole squad belaying and encouraging.

Low ropes

Ground-level problem solving that needs the group to work as one.

Canoes

On the water together, off the field entirely.

$30 per player

Priced separately and not included in the camp fee. Your team chooses one activity.

Mountain Challenge is optional. Let camp staff know when you register if your team would like to take part.

More than soccer

Maryville sits about 25 minutes from downtown Knoxville and 25 minutes from Great Smoky Mountains National Park, which makes camp week an easy team trip as well as a preseason.

If your team would like to add a hike in the mountains or a trip to Gatlinburg around camp, camp staff are happy to help you plan it.
