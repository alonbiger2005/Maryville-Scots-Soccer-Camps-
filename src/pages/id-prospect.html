What the day looks like

ID & Prospect Camp is built for college-bound players who want to know what a college program actually feels like from the inside.

The Maryville College staff run a college-style practice and small-sided games, and personally evaluate every player who attends. You will meet current players and alumni who can tell you what the jump from high school to college soccer really involves.

You will also learn the “Maryville Starting 11” — the core values that guide the program — along with the system of play the college teams use and how a player like you would fit into it.

The day includes

  • A college-style training session led by Maryville College staff
  • Small-sided and full-sided games
  • Personal evaluation by the coaching staff
  • Time with current players and alumni
  • The Maryville Starting 11 and the system of play
  • An open Q&A session
  • Lunch on campus
