Why Maryville Soccer Camp?

College-level coaching

Sessions are led by experienced college and high school coaches, alongside current Maryville College players.

Player development

Technical work, tactical understanding and a genuinely competitive training environment across every camp.

A campus worth visiting

Maryville College sits 20 miles from Knoxville and minutes from the Great Smoky Mountains.

More than a camp

Campers meet the values and the system of play that guide the Maryville College soccer program.

Camp is run by the staff of the Maryville College men’s and women’s soccer programs. Campers train on the same fields the college teams use, and hear from the players who play there.

Train in the heart of East Tennessee

Maryville College was founded in 1819 and is consistently ranked among the top liberal arts colleges in the South. The campus sits about 20 miles from Knoxville and minutes from the Great Smoky Mountains National Park.

Every session, meal and overnight stay happens on campus, so families always know where their camper is.
